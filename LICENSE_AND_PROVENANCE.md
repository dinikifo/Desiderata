## AI-assisted development statement

Desiderata was developed through substantial **human-directed collaboration with generative AI systems**. AI systems contributed to theoretical exploration, architectural criticism, implementation, test creation, defect detection and documentation. Human participants reviewed, directed and authorized changes. Some reviews and checks were AI-assisted and must not be described as independent human adjudication. The identities or providers of the systems used are not a prerequisite for evaluating the released source; technical provenance instead depends on source versions, diffs, hashes, test receipts and review status.

## Experimental status

The implementation is a bounded, local prototype. Formal results are scoped to their declared predicates and evidence. Scientific efficacy, complete RFC invariant discharge, secure agent delegation, platform independence and general truth convergence have **not** been established. Consequential-use `HOLD` is an explicit project recommendation; it is **not a CC0 restriction on downstream legal reuse** and not a technical safety barrier.
